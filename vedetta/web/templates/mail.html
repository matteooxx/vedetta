Why this is narrow on purpose

Of the applications in a typical search, most end in silence: a posting closing tells you nothing, and a tracker row stays open forever. Mail is the one place an outcome reliably lands — which is also why reaching into it has to be bounded.

So this never writes to your own application records. Those are hand-written and carry reasoning no status word holds, and a program guessing “rejected” from a phrase and editing them would be vandalism. It proposes here; you decide; your records stay yours.
